2. User rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to processing of personal data; withdraw consent for optional integrations; and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

3. How to make a request

Send requests to info@sales-card.com. For security, we may ask users to verify account ownership before acting on a request. Users should send requests from the email address associated with their SalesCard account where possible.

4. Legal bases

  • Contract: to provide the app and account features requested by the user.
  • Consent: for optional integrations, permissions, and selected features.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure the service, prevent misuse, improve reliability, and provide support.
  • Legal obligations: for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, compliance, and app-store obligations where applicable.

5. Optional integrations

Google and Microsoft integrations are optional. Users can disconnect integrations from SalesCard settings where available or from the relevant Google or Microsoft account permission settings. Disconnecting an integration may limit calendar, meeting, or email-assisted features.

6. Data minimization, retention, and deletion

SalesCard aims to request and use only the data needed to provide selected features. Data is kept only as long as needed for the app, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or support. Deletion requests are handled according to the Delete Account page.

7. Contact

For all privacy and data-protection matters, contact info@sales-card.com.
