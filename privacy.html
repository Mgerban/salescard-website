Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 May 2026

Important: This is a launch-ready draft for the website and app-store review. Before commercial launch, replace the legal placeholder and have the final text reviewed by a qualified lawyer.

1. Who we are

SalesCard is a mobile-first CRM application that helps users capture business contacts, manage leads, organize follow-ups, schedule sales activities, and understand relationship engagement.

Contact: info@sales-card.com

Controller / operator: [Insert legal name and business address before launch]

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, authentication method, account status, profile image where provided, language, and user settings.
  • Contact and lead data: business contact names, companies, roles, email addresses, phone numbers, social links, notes, tags, reminders, tasks, lead status, relationship context, and pipeline activity entered or imported by the user.
  • Business card and import data: images or text extracted from business cards, CSV/vCard imports, QR/NFC contact data, and related fields when the user chooses to use these features.
  • Calendar and email integration data: calendar availability, event metadata, invite details, attendees, time zones, email workflow metadata, templates, and user-selected follow-up context where the user authorizes Google or Microsoft access.
  • Technical data: device type, operating system, app version, crash logs, diagnostic logs, IP address, security logs, and performance data.
  • Subscription and support data: app-store subscription status, purchase identifiers, support messages, issue reports, and screenshots shared by the user.

3. How we use information

  • To create, authenticate, secure, and manage accounts.
  • To provide contact capture, lead management, reminders, pipeline, notes, scheduling, import, and follow-up features.
  • To provide user-authorized Google and Microsoft integrations such as sign-in, calendar availability, meeting creation, and email-assisted follow-up workflows.
  • To personalize the app experience, including language, time zone, default calendar platform, and saved preferences.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse, troubleshoot bugs, improve reliability, provide support, and comply with legal and platform obligations.

4. Legal bases

Where GDPR or similar laws apply, we process personal data based on performance of a contract, consent, legitimate interests, legal obligations, and security/service integrity. Optional integrations are based on user authorization and can be disconnected.

5. Google API data

If users connect a Google account, SalesCard uses Google user data only to provide or improve user-facing features visible in the app, such as sign-in, calendar availability, meeting creation, and email-assisted workflows selected by the user. SalesCard’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

SalesCard does not sell Google user data, does not use Google user data for advertising, and does not allow humans to read Google user data except where necessary for security, support requested by the user, legal compliance, or service operation.

6. Microsoft data

If users connect a Microsoft account, SalesCard uses Microsoft account and Microsoft Graph data only for user-facing features the user authorizes, such as sign-in, calendar availability, meeting creation, and productivity workflows. Users can revoke access from SalesCard settings where available or from their Microsoft account permissions page.

7. Sharing and processors

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with service providers that help operate SalesCard, such as hosting, authentication, database infrastructure, email delivery, calendar/email integration providers, crash reporting, analytics, customer support, payment processors, and app-store platforms. These providers process data only as needed to provide their services or meet legal and security obligations.

8. Retention

We keep personal data as long as needed to provide SalesCard, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Account and lead data are generally kept while the account is active. Some logs, support records, transaction records, or backups may remain for limited periods after deletion where required.

9. User rights and deletion

Depending on location, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to processing of personal data. Users may also withdraw consent for optional integrations. Requests can be sent to info@sales-card.com. See also the Data Protection page and Delete Account page.

10. Security, children, and changes

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data. SalesCard is intended for professional use and is not directed to children. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time by posting the updated version on this page.

11. Contact

Questions, privacy requests, and deletion requests can be sent to info@sales-card.com.
