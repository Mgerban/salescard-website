Delete Account

Last updated: 18 May 2026

How to request deletion

Users can request deletion of their SalesCard account and associated personal data by emailing info@sales-card.com.

What to include

  • Use the email address connected to the SalesCard account where possible.
  • Use the subject line: Delete my SalesCard account.
  • Include the account email address and login method, such as email, Google, or Microsoft.

What happens after a request

We may verify account ownership before deleting data. Once verified, we will delete or anonymize account data and personal data associated with the account unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute resolution, or app-store obligations.

Data that may be deleted

  • Account profile information.
  • Contacts, leads, notes, reminders, tasks, and pipeline records associated with the account.
  • User-created sales workflow data.
  • Connected integration tokens where applicable.

Disconnecting Google or Microsoft access

Users can also revoke SalesCard’s access directly from their Google or Microsoft account security/permissions settings. Revoking access limits future access but does not automatically delete the SalesCard account unless a deletion request is also made.

Contact

Deletion requests and questions can be sent to info@sales-card.com.
